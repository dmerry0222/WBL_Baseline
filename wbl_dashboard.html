Baseline Engagement in Work-Based Learning

For the 1,107 undergraduate students of the 2026 graduating cohort. A synthesis of LinkedIn research, student resume reviews, patchwork departmental data about internships, and class registration information from OIRA.

For general extrapolation purposes only. Suffolk lacks a centralized system for this information, so the data are somewhat rough. That will tend to err on the side of omitting great experiences that were not found, rather than overestimating them.
